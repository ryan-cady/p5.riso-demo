# Available RISO Ink Colors

These are the ink drums currently available in our print shop. When planning your layers, keep in mind that each color requires a separate pass through the machine.

## Notes

- **1-drum colors** are available as single-color prints
- **2-drum colors** can be combined for two-color prints — pick any two from the list
- Some colors appear in both lists (Yellow, Teal, Aqua)
- Use these exact strings when calling `new Riso()` in your sketches
- Preview how colors look and overlap at the [p5.riso site](https://antiboredom.github.io/p5.riso/)
